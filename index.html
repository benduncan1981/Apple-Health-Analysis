Apple Health Easy Dashboard

Step 1: Upload your export.xml

Select your Apple Health export file (export.xml) below. The system will safely stream and parse it.

Step 2: Compare Metrics

Select two metrics from your Apple Health data to compare. You can also scan all correlations.

Step 3: Add Timeline Events

Add events such as medication start, illness, or stress periods. These will overlay on charts.

Step 4: Visualize Data

Line chart and scatter chart of selected metrics. Timeline events are shown below charts.

Step 5: Strongest Correlations

Click “Scan All Correlations” to see the top correlations between all metrics.
